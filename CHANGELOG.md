# Changelog / 版本记录

## 0.2.0

- English and Simplified Chinese are available throughout the app, including the floating window and exported PNG reports. Android 13 and later support selecting the app language in system settings.
- The checklist and report now use check-in wording consistently. The 14-day indicator counts local app launch check-ins.
- The repository includes English and Chinese documentation, privacy information, and contribution instructions.

- 主界面、悬浮窗和导出 PNG 均支持简体中文和英文。Android 13 及以上可在系统设置中选择应用语言。
- 清单和报告统一使用打卡用语。14 天进度按本机应用启动打卡累计。
- 仓库增加了中英文使用文档、隐私说明和贡献指南。
